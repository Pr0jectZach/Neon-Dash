// 01 — ABOUT THE GAME

WHAT IS NEON DASH?

🎮

FLUID PLATFORMING

Tight, responsive controls with double-jump mechanics. Move, jump, and stomp your way through 3 handcrafted neon levels.

⚡

GLOWING VISUALS

Every pixel pulses with neon light. Animated trail effects, particle explosions, and a scrolling cyberpunk cityscape backdrop.

👾

ENEMY AI

Patrol enemies move dynamically across platforms. Land on top of them to stomp for bonus points — but one wrong move costs a life.

🏆

HIGHSCORE SYSTEM

Your top 5 scores are saved locally. Enter your name after each run and chase the top of the leaderboard.

🔮

PORTAL SYSTEM

The exit portal only activates once every coin is collected. Grab them all first — then race to the goal.

💻

PURE WEB TECH

100% HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No libraries, no frameworks, no installs. Open in any browser and play instantly.
